\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented the first Byzantine-robust federated learning protocol with formal practical
self-stabilization guarantees~\cite{arora_gouda,dolev_kat_practical}: from any bounded
initialization ($F(w^0)-F^*\leq\Delta_0$, Assumption~6), the protocol reaches a
legitimate configuration within $T^* = O(\Delta_0(\sigma^2\beta^2/\varepsilon^2 + \beta^2/\varepsilon))$
rounds and converges at $O(\sigma\beta/\sqrt{T} + \beta^2/T)$, with the BBP phase
transition at $\sigma^2\beta^2=0.25$ providing a matching detection impossibility
\cite{bbp_transition,byzantine_ml}.
Empirical evaluation across 12 attack types achieves 78.4\% accuracy at 40\% Byzantine
fraction---15 points above FLTrust~\cite{crfl}.
Open problems: fully decentralized spectral aggregation, asynchronous self-stabilization,
DP integration preserving the phase-transition condition, and tight Non-IID concentration
bounds (Appendix~\ref{app:game_theory}).
