\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Coordinator trust.}
The coordinator is assumed non-Byzantine; fully decentralized spectral aggregation
without a trusted coordinator remains open.

\textbf{Phase transition.}
Detection fails at $\sigma^2\beta^2 \geq 0.25$; DP or trusted validation sets
(Appendix~\ref{app:game_theory}) can extend the feasible regime.

\textbf{Robustness boundaries.}
$O(1/\sqrt{k})$ sketch error~\cite{ghashami_fd} may mask spikes near $\lambda_+$; low-rank
coordinated attacks reduce detection to 73.2\% (cross-layer mitigates); heavy-tailed
gradients~\cite{rmt_modern}, dynamic Byzantine client sets~\cite{bft_consensus}, full
asynchrony, and DP integration preserving the phase-transition condition remain open.
