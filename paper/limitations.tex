\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Coordinator trust.}
The coordinator is assumed non-Byzantine; a fully decentralized spectral aggregation
without a trusted coordinator remains open.

\textbf{Phase transition.}
Detection fails at $\sigma^2\beta^2 \geq 0.25$; DP or trusted validation sets
(Appendix~\ref{app:game_theory}) can extend the feasible regime.

\textbf{Sketching.}
$O(1/\sqrt{k})$ eigenvalue error~\cite{ghashami_fd} requires $k \geq 256$; very small
spikes near $\lambda_+$ may cause false negatives.

\textbf{Attack variants.}
Block-targeted low-rank attacks reduce detection to 73.2\% (cross-layer mitigates);
heavy-tailed gradient distributions may weaken the MP universality argument~\cite{rmt_modern}.

\textbf{Dynamic membership, asynchrony, and privacy.}
Extending to dynamic Byzantine client sets~\cite{bft_consensus}, full asynchrony, and
formal differential privacy while preserving the phase-transition condition remain open.
